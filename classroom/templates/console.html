What the buttons do

  • Start — go2-student.sh create (first time) then up: builds the image if needed and starts their container.
  • Stop — down. Their work is a bind mount, so nothing is lost.
  • Sync — hands out updated coursework. The example notebook is read-only, so this replaces it in place.
  • Forget — removes them from this list only. Their container, their work folder and their port stay exactly where they are; cleaning those up is a decision for someone at a terminal.
