Terms of use

This site is run by CompassPoint Mentorship for students in its programmes. Using it means agreeing to the following, which is short on purpose.

🌼 In one line

It is a teaching tool, free to use for people in the programme, offered as-is, and the link we give you is yours alone.

Who it is for

Students and mentors in CompassPoint Mentorship programmes. Accounts are created by a mentor; there is no public sign-up.

Your sign-in link is a password

A join code, and the link to a student lab, sign you in by themselves. Do not post them or share them. Tell your mentor if one gets out and we will issue a new one.

Using it sensibly

  • Use your own account and your own lab, not someone else's.
  • Do not try to reach other students' work, the server itself, or any part of the system you were not given.
  • Do not use the lab to attack, scan or disrupt anything, here or elsewhere.
  • Do not upload anything unlawful, or anything you do not have the right to share.
  • Running your own code in your own lab is the entire point — experiment freely there.

A mentor can stop a lab or remove an account if any of this is ignored.

The dog is simulated

Nothing on this site drives a physical robot. The real-robot mode exists in the software and is switched off on this server by design. Hardware sessions, when they happen, are run in person with an adult present and a remote in hand.

Your work

The code a student writes stays theirs. We keep it on the server so it is there next session, and we will delete it on request — see Privacy. Keep your own copy of anything you care about; this is a classroom server, not a backup service.

The software

The simulator, the course material and this site are open source, BSD-3-Clause, at github.com/CompassPoint-Mentorship/MuJoCo-Go2. You are welcome to read it, copy it and run your own.

No warranty

This is a free teaching tool offered as-is. We cannot promise it will be available at any particular moment, and we are not liable for work lost to a failure, a restart or a mistake. We try hard, and we will tell you plainly when something breaks.
