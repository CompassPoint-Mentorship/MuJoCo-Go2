Privacy

What this site stores, why, and how to have it removed. Written plainly because the people using it are teenagers and their parents.

🌼 The short version

We keep the least we can: a name, sometimes an email address, and the coursework a student writes. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and there are no trackers or analytics on this site.

What is stored

WhatWhyWhere
A student's name, as their mentor typed it To label their lab and their work folder A database file on CompassPoint's own server
A join code So a student can sign in without a Google account The same database
An email address — only if the mentor adds one So that student can sign in with Google instead of a code The same database
For a mentor signing in with Google: the address and display name on that Google account To check they are on the mentor list, and to show who added a student The same database and a cookie in their browser
The code a student writes in their notebooks It is their work; it is kept so it is there next session A folder on the same server
Ordinary web server logs — address, page, time To see that the site is working and to investigate faults The server and its network provider

Signing in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells this site three things: that the sign-in succeeded, your email address, and the name on the account. That is all we ask for and all we receive — we cannot read your mail, your files, your contacts or anything else, and no password ever reaches this site.

Google's own handling of that sign-in is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can use a join code instead and skip Google entirely.

Cookies

One cookie, called session. It holds who you are signed in as and nothing else, it is signed so it cannot be edited, and it expires after two weeks. There are no advertising or analytics cookies on this site, first-party or otherwise.

The microphone

The course has an exercise where the robot dog repeats what you say. That recording is made by your browser, is held in memory on the lab server so it can be played back, and is discarded when the lab restarts. It is never written to disk here and never leaves the server. Nothing records anything unless you press record or run a cell that asks for it.

Who can see it

Mentors running the class, and the person who administers the server. The simulated dog is software; no robot is connected to this site. Student work lives in that student's own container and is not visible to other students.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or to train anything.

How long, and how to have it deleted

Roster entries last as long as the course. Student work is kept so students can come back to it, and is removed on request or when the course ends.

To see what is held about you, or to have it deleted, email tech@cpmentorship.org. If you are a parent asking on behalf of your child, say so and we will act on it — we do not require the student to ask.

Children

This site is used by students in CompassPoint Mentorship programmes, including under-13s. It collects no more than what is listed above, asks for nothing about a student beyond a name, and a student can take part with no email address at all by using a join code. Parents may ask for their child's information to be removed at any time at the address above.

Changes

If this changes, the page changes with it. Last updated 13 November 2026. Questions: tech@cpmentorship.org, CompassPoint Mentorship.
