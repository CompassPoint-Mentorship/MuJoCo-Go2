Go2 Simulator — installing on a Mac
===================================

Double-click  "Install Go2 Simulator.command"


macOS will probably refuse the first time
-----------------------------------------
You may see:

    "Install Go2 Simulator.command" cannot be opened because it is
    from an unidentified developer.

That is macOS being careful about files from the internet, not a sign
that anything is wrong. Two ways past it:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) the file, choose Open, then Open
     again in the box that appears.

  2. If macOS still refuses: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll to the bottom, and click "Open Anyway" next to the message
     about this file. Then double-click it again.

You only have to do this once.


The warning-free alternative
----------------------------
If you are comfortable with Terminal, this skips the warning entirely,
because files fetched by curl are not quarantined. Open Terminal
(press Cmd-Space, type Terminal, press Return), paste this one line and
press Return:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CompassPoint-Mentorship/MuJoCo-Go2/main/install/mac.sh | bash


What gets installed
-------------------
Everything lands in two folders you own. Nothing system-wide, and you
are never asked for a password.

  ~/.go2                            the simulator and its own Python
  ~/Applications/Go2 Simulator.app  the thing you double-click to start
  ~/Documents/Go2 Simulator         your notebooks (yours to keep)

To remove it later, run  ~/.go2/bin/go2-uninstall  in Terminal. Your
notebooks are not touched.


Requirements
------------
  macOS 11 or newer, and WiFi for the install (about 250 MB).
  Apple Silicon (M1 and later) or Intel; both work.
